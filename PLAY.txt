# Idle Fantacia

An idle RPG that runs entirely in your browser. No install, no account, no server.

## Play

Open `index.html`, or serve the folder:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

then visit <http://localhost:8000>.

## Where saves live

In your browser's local storage, per site. That means:

- Saves stay on the device and browser you played on.
- Clearing site data clears them.
- To move a save, use **ส่งออก / นำเข้า** on the profile screen — it writes a file you can carry
  to another browser or machine.

## Offline

Everything is in the folder — no fonts, scripts or images are fetched from anywhere else. Once the
page has loaded it keeps working with the network off.
